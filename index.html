About SYNEX

Fostering Groundbreaking Solutions For Real-World Challenges

SYNEX 2026 is an intensive, high-energy hackathon designed to empower student innovators and tech pioneers. Over 36 exhilarating hours, teams will conceptualize, build, and deploy production-ready prototypes across 8 multidisciplinary domains.

Whether you are an experienced systems programmer, an AI enthusiast, an IoT hardware tinkerer, or a product designer, SYNEX provides the ideal launchpad to validate your hypotheses with veteran industry mentors and venture scouts.

Guidelines Preview

Participation Rules at a Glance

Fair play, academic integrity, and collaboration are fundamental to the spirit of SYNEX.

Eligibility Criteria

Open to all currently enrolled undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students from recognized institutions across India.

Team Structure

Individual entries or teams consisting of 2 to 4 members. Inter-college and inter-departmental teams are warmly welcomed.

Originality & Code

All code must be authored during the 36-hour sprint. Open-source libraries are permitted, but pre-built proprietary apps are banned.

100% IP Ownership

Participants retain complete, unencumbered ownership of all intellectual property, source code, and assets developed during the event.

Code of Conduct

We foster a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of gender, identity, or background.

Strict Deadlines

Git repositories and project submission forms must be finalized before the 05:00 PM code freeze on Day 2.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating, registrations, logistics, and eligibility.

Any student currently enrolled in an undergraduate, postgraduate, or research program in engineering, design, management, or pure sciences is eligible. Recent graduates (class of 2026) are also welcome with a valid student ID.

Both options are supported! You can register as an Individual (₹299) if you wish to hack solo, or as a Team of 2 to 4 members (₹999 total). We also host a virtual team-formation mixer on Discord prior to kickoff for solo participants looking to form teams.

Registration fees cover official kits, venue amenities, continuous meals, and infrastructure. Due to advance logistics bookings, entry fees are non-refundable. However, team member substitutions can be requested up to 72 hours before the event.

Bring your laptop, chargers, extension cords, hardware development boards (if competing in IoT track), a valid college identification card, and personal toiletries. High-speed Wi-Fi, power ports, food, refreshments, and sleeping zones are arranged.

Submissions are handled via our portal by providing a public GitHub repository with commit history, a live deploy URL or APK, and a brief pitch deck. Our judging rubric awards 25% for Innovation, 25% for Technical Complexity, 20% for Practical Viability, 15% for User Experience, and 15% for Pitch Quality.

Yes! SYNEX 2026 offers a hybrid track. Remote participants can join mentoring calls and jury presentations via Zoom/Discord while enjoying parity in judging criteria and special online category awards.
