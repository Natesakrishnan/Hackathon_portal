1

Eligibility & Format

SYNEX 2026 is open to all students actively pursuing full-time or part-time undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc), postgraduate (M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc, MBA), or diploma degrees from accredited educational institutions worldwide.

Both in-person attendance at our Bengaluru tech campus and virtual hybrid participation options are provided. All participants must present a valid student identity card or proof of current enrollment at check-in.

2

Individual & Team Composition

Participants may choose to compete individually or in teams of up to 4 members:

  • Solo Hackers: Individuals can register at the discounted individual rate of ₹{{ individual_fee }}.
  • Teams: Teams can comprise between 2 to 4 members with a fixed team entry fee of ₹{{ team_fee }}.
  • Inter-College Teams: Cross-institutional teams and interdisciplinary pairings (e.g. Computer Science + Healthcare/Biotech) are highly encouraged.
  • Single Entry Restriction: An individual can belong to only one team during the event.
3

Project Originality & Academic Integrity

All core codebase, architectural designs, and functional components must be authored during the official 36-hour hacking sprint. Pre-existing applications, pre-built turnkey templates, or previously published capstone projects are strictly prohibited.

Use of publicly available open-source libraries, frameworks (React, Flutter, PyTorch, Node.js), public APIs, and foundational models is permitted provided they are disclosed in your project README file.

4

100% Intellectual Property (IP) Retention

You own what you build. Neither the SYNEX organizers, sponsors, nor venue partners claim any rights, equity, or ownership over the intellectual property created during the hackathon.

Teams remain free to open-source, patent, incorporate into startups, or commercialize their products after the event concludes.

5

Code of Conduct

SYNEX is committed to providing a safe, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

Harassment of any kind, plagiarism, toxic competitive behavior, or violation of venue regulations will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the premises without refund.

6

Submission Requirements

To qualify for judging, all teams must finalize and submit the following assets prior to the Day 2 05:00 PM code freeze:

  • GitHub Repository: Public link containing all source code with commit history timestamped during the sprint.
  • README Documentation: Clear setup instructions, problem statement, architecture overview, and third-party dependencies.
  • Demonstration Video: A 2-minute unedited walkthrough screen recording showcasing live functionality.
  • Pitch Deck: 5-slide PDF presentation outlining value proposition, market impact, and technical stack.
7

Evaluation & Judging Rubric

Projects will be systematically evaluated by an independent jury of engineering directors, venture capitalists, and domain specialists according to a weighted rubric:

Evaluation Parameter Weight Key Focus Points
Innovation & Originality 25% Novelty of approach, creativity, and unconventional problem solving.
Technical Complexity 25% Architecture quality, algorithmic depth, performance, and code hygiene.
Practical Viability & Impact 20% Real-world feasibility, market relevance, and potential for societal adoption.
User Experience (UI/UX) 15% Intuitive interface, accessibility standards, responsive ergonomics.
Presentation & Pitch 15% Clarity of communication, handling of jury Q&A, demo readiness.
8

Entry Fee & Refund Policy

Registration fees contribute directly towards venue infrastructure, 1Gbps connectivity, official kits, mentor travel, and round-the-clock catering for participants:

  • Individual Fee: ₹{{ individual_fee }}
  • Team Fee: ₹{{ team_fee }} (fixed fee for 2 to 4 members)
Non-Refundable Notice: Because logistics, meals, and swag production are locked in advance with vendors, registration fees are non-refundable. Team substitutions can be approved up to 72 hours before kickoff.

  • Team Roster Freeze: November 12, 2026 at 06:00 PM IST
  • Hackathon Kickoff: November 14, 2026 at 08:30 AM IST
  • Final Code Freeze: November 15, 2026 at 05:00 PM IST
